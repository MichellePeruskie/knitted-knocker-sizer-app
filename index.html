Knitted Knockers Sizer

Enter the recipient's bra size to check whether the requested Knocker size is appropriate for that band size. The tool uses the Knitted Knockers bra sizing chart as a guardrail for unusual band/cup combinations.

Most recipients will receive the cup size they requested. The recommendation changes only when the charted bra diameter differs from the standard finished Knocker diameter by more than 1 inch.

You can find approved patterns and additional sizing information on the Knitted Knockers Downloads page.

This tool follows the bra-size and cup-diameter relationships listed on the Knitted Knockers sizing chart. It keeps the requested cup as the default and adjusts only clear outlier combinations rather than trying to make the finished Knocker fill the entire bra cup.

If a size is not listed on the chart or cannot be mapped safely to a standard Knitted Knockers pattern size, contact Knitted Knockers for sizing guidance.

Knitted Knockers is a volunteer-run nonprofit whose mission is to connect knitters and crocheters with breast cancer survivors. Learn more.
